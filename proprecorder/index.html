Bars are unique stations heard by PSKReporter receivers in the grid in each 15-minute slot (mostly FT8), scaled per band. A gap means no data, not a dead band. Busy weekends and contests raise the counts, so compare a band with itself across days.

LOG A BAND SWEEP

Fill only the bands you checked. SSB and FT8 only (CW falls between them). 30m is skipped for SSB (no phone there). Time is your computer's local time; it is stored in UTC.

HOW WELL DOES EACH RATING MATCH?

Median DX stations heard per 15-minute slot, by PropMon's rating at the time, and the share of slots with at least one DX station. Slots with no receiver in the grid on that band are left out. If "good" and "fair" look the same for a band, its thresholds are worth a look.

YOUR SWEEPS vs PROPMON

Scale (decided 2026-11-07): GOOD = DX on SSB · FAIR = DX on FT8 only, or NA only on SSB · POOR = NA only or nothing even on FT8. A single sweep can allow two ratings (e.g. FT8 DX = good or fair). PropMon's rating is taken from the recorded slot nearest the sweep (within 15 minutes).
